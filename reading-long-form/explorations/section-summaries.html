Collapse sections into summaries

Today’s fold reads like a paywall: the section fades out under a floating white card with a button, the same shape as “subscribe to keep reading”. Three color treatments keep the fold but drop that look. Three UX treatments rethink it. Each one uses section 5 and opens on a click.

Current

For comparison. Faded text, then a white card that floats over it with a shadow and a button.

Color 1 · Paper

No card. The summary is a band of the page itself across the column, between two rules, like a pull quote set into the fold rather than something laid on top of it.

Color 2 · Teal

Flat, and in the teal that Emphasize key information uses for key sentences, text included. It reads as one of the reading ideas at work, not a gate.

Color 3 · Grey

The same soft grey as the … pills on collapsed text, with no border or shadow: the whole section becomes one big collapsed run.

UX 1 · Standfirst

No fade and no card. The summary sits under the heading like a magazine standfirst, in my voice, and a plain line below it opens the section, with how long it is.

UX 2 · Outline

Nothing sits behind a card. The summary leads, then every paragraph shrinks to its first sentence with a … pill, so you see the shape of the argument and open any part of it.

UX 3 · Margin note

Nothing is hidden. The section stays open and the summary sits beside it in the margin, staying in view as you read. On a phone it sits under the heading.
